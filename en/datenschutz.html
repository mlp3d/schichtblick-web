2. The key points in brief

This policy applies to the “Schichtblick” app on all platforms on which we offer it (Android as well as iPhone and iPad). Schichtblick is deliberately built so that we never get to see your data: there is no server for it to flow to. There is no user account, no sign-in and no server on which we store your shift or pay data. Through the app we collect no personal data about you, show no advertising and use no analytics, tracking or advertising services. We do not create usage profiles. We only receive personal data if you write to us yourself, see section 9.

3. Local processing on your device

All content you create in Schichtblick (your shifts, shift types and shift patterns, your base wage (Grundlohn), the supplement rates (Zuschlagssätze) you have entered, pay rate and allowance profiles, monthly allowances, your setting for when supplements are paid out, your breaks, times when you left early, your rule for crediting hours to your time account (Zeitkonto) and the values derived from all this) is stored and processed on your device; we do not run a server and never receive this data. For the backup made by your operating system, see section 4. The calculation of the supplements and of the shares free of income tax (Lohnsteuer) also runs entirely on your device. No data is transferred to us in the process. With Schichtblick+ you can place a widget on your home screen. It shows your next shifts with name, date and time and is therefore visible to anyone who looks at your screen. It does not show vacation and sick days, the types you have deselected for calendar export, or appointments from the family area. For this, the app keeps an intermediate file with your upcoming shifts on your device. It is excluded from your operating system's backup, and the app does not pass it on. Data only leaves the app by routes you choose yourself: when you share or export something (section 7) and when you set up the family area (section 8). In addition, there is the backup by your operating system (section 4). A local backup you create (export as a file) is stored wherever you save it. If you wish, you can protect this file with a password when exporting; it is then encrypted on your device. Only you know the password; it is not stored anywhere and not transmitted.

Legal basis and purposes: Insofar as we are responsible for the processing on your device because, through the app, we decide on its purposes and means, we base it on Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. b DSGVO. The purpose in each case is the very function you use: keeping your shift schedule, the calculation of the supplements and of the shares free of income tax (Lohnsteuer), reminding you of your shifts, creating backups, reports, images and files that you pass on yourself, and the family area under section 8. Without this processing, the function in question would not exist. Storing this information on your device and accessing it are strictly necessary for the service you have expressly requested, § 25 Abs. 2 Nr. 2 TDDDG. For calendar access in the family area, what is stated in section 8 also applies.

Whether you provide information is up to you: you are under no legal or contractual obligation to provide us with data, and we do not require any for using the app. If you do not enter your base wage or your supplement rates, the app uses a placeholder and tells you so. If you do not grant a permission, Schichtblick remains usable; only the affected function is then unavailable. If you do not write to us, no data about you arises on our side.

4. Backup by the operating system

So that your data survives a change of device, Schichtblick is included in your operating system's backup function. On Android this is Android's automatic backup, which places the app's data in your Google account; on iOS it is your iPhone's device backup to iCloud or to your computer.

This backup is carried out not by Schichtblick but by your operating system, and it goes into your own account or storage area. We receive no data in the process and have no access to the backup. The privacy policies of Google or Apple apply to its processing. You can turn off the backup in your device's settings.

On Android, the operating system encrypts this backup with a key derived from your screen lock. If you have not set up a screen lock, Schichtblick's data is not backed up to the cloud at all.

5. Schichtblick+ and payment processing

Schichtblick+ is a one-time purchase that unlocks the app's extended functions. The purchase is handled entirely through the respective app store: for the App Store by Apple Distribution International Ltd. (Hollyhill Industrial Estate, Hollyhill, Cork, Ireland), for Google Play by Google Ireland Limited (Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland). These providers process your payment data under their own responsibility in accordance with their own privacy policies. At no time do we receive your payment data (such as your credit card number) or any information about your person. To check and restore your purchase via “Restore purchases” (“Käufe wiederherstellen”), the app communicates directly with the store, without us placing our own server or any other service provider in between.

Free trial: On iOS you start it via a free item in the App Store. Apple handles it like a purchase and lists it in your purchase history. The app reads only the start time from it. On Android, the free trial does not run through Google Play. On both platforms, the app stores the start and the most recently seen time of your device only on your device. Both are included in your operating system's backup (section 4) so that reinstalling does not start a new free trial. We do not receive this information. The legal basis is Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. b DSGVO, because the one-time free trial is part of the offer that you accept by starting it. Storing and reading on your device are strictly necessary for this, § 25 Abs. 2 Nr. 2 TDDDG.

6. App permissions

  • Internet: only for the store. At startup, the app quietly asks the store whether your purchase is still valid and, on iOS, when your free trial began. If the store reports that the purchase was refunded, the app locks Schichtblick+ again; if it does not answer, for example without a network, nothing changes. The app also needs the connection for the purchase, for starting the free trial on iOS and for “Restore purchases” (“Käufe wiederherstellen”). The free trial on Android needs no connection. Your shift and pay data are not transferred to us. If you have set up the family area, Schichtblick enters your shifts into your device's calendar. They only reach the internet because your operating system syncs this calendar with your account. That is done not by the app but by your device. Details in section 8.
  • File access: only when you yourself save a file or have one read in: a backup, a PDF report, a shared month image, a plan for colleagues, a shift swap offer or a calendar file. Access is limited to the file you select for this. The same applies the other way round when you tap a Schichtblick file in another app (for example in a chat or an email) and choose Schichtblick to open it: the app makes a copy of this one file in its own storage, reads it there, shows you what it contains before you accept it, and then deletes the copy again. The original stays unchanged. If the app does not recognise the file as its own, it rejects it.
  • Calendar: only when you open the family area. For this, Schichtblick requests read and write access to your device's calendar in order to enter your shifts into the calendar you choose, to show you the appointments from the shared calendar you choose, and to create, change and delete appointments there. The title, time period, location, note and reminders of an appointment are read. As long as you do not open the family area, the app never accesses your calendar. What happens in detail is described in section 8.
  • Notifications: only when you turn on reminders for your shifts. The reminder is scheduled and triggered on your device. Nothing goes to us or to any push service for this. The legal basis is Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. b DSGVO. You can turn off the reminders in the app at any time and withdraw the permission in your device's settings.

7. Sharing your shift schedule

When you share a month, the app creates the image entirely on your device and hands it to your device's sharing function (for example Messages, email or a social network). When sharing, the app sends nothing on its own; it only hands the image to the app you choose. Schichtblick writes on its own in only one place, namely in the family area into the calendar you have chosen for it (section 8). Who receives the image is decided by you alone through the app you choose for sharing. We do not run a server for this and store nothing.

The same applies to the two other files you can pass on: the plan for colleagues and the calendar file for other calendar apps. They too are created entirely on your device, and you decide with each export what goes into them. The plan for colleagues contains no vacation or sick days and no information about earnings. It includes the list of your shift types, and therefore their names as well. For the calendar file, you choose yourself before each export which types are included.

A shift swap offer contains a single shift: date, shift type with name, abbreviation, colour, symbol and times, if you wish the day you are looking to have off in return, and a name and a note if you type them in yourself. It contains no information about earnings; vacation and sick days cannot be offered. Whoever opens the file first sees a preview. The shift is only accepted when they tap, and only into their own plan. When sharing, the app adds a link to our website to its accompanying text. Anyone who taps it visits our website; section 13 applies to that.

8. Family area: access to your device's calendar

The family area is part of Schichtblick+. It is not switched on by default and stays inactive as long as you do not open and set it up yourself. When you open it for the first time, Schichtblick explains on a separate screen what calendar access is needed for, and only then requests it through your operating system. This happens before any purchase offer is shown to you. If you decline, Schichtblick remains fully usable; only the family area is then unavailable.

Next, you choose from your device's calendars the one into which your shifts should be entered. If you do not yet have a shared calendar, you can choose “Create new calendar” (“Neuen Kalender anlegen”) at this point; this opens your browser on a Google page. Creating the calendar and sending invitations happen there, not in Schichtblick, and Google's terms apply to that. Which shared calendar is shown in the plan is chosen separately, under “More” (“Mehr”) in the “Family” (“Familie”) area. As long as you do not do that, Schichtblick reads no appointments.

Legal basis. We base the processing in the family area on Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. b DSGVO: without it, the function that is part of Schichtblick+ would not exist. Access to the calendar information already stored on your device is strictly necessary for this service you have expressly requested (§ 25 Abs. 2 Nr. 2 TDDDG); in addition, you expressly approve it via your operating system's prompt, which is preceded by the explanation in the app (§ 25 Abs. 1 TDDDG). You can withdraw this approval at any time in your device's settings. For consent (Einwilligung) under Art. 9 DSGVO, see the paragraph on sick days.

What Schichtblick writes. Into the calendar you choose for this, the app enters your own shifts. What is passed on is the name of the shift type, which you gave it yourself, as well as start and end, or the day for all-day entries such as vacation. The period written runs from the first of the current month to 90 days into the future. The app syncs it as soon as you open it or change something in your plan. Your base wage, your supplement rates and everything derived from them are left out. Schichtblick does not enter any amount of money into a calendar on its own, and adds no note, location or reminder to the mirrored shifts. What you type into the fields of an appointment yourself, however, goes into the calendar just as you enter it.

You decide what is included. You choose which shift types are transferred in the same selection as for calendar export. If you deselect a type, Schichtblick removes the already entered appointments of that type from your calendar again at the next sync.

Sick days and other health information. The shift type “Sick” (“Krank”) is deselected from the start, because a calendar account is often linked to work as well. If you deliberately include “Sick” or a type of your own for days of illness, that is information about your health and therefore belongs to the special categories of personal data under Art. 9 Abs. 1 DSGVO. By ticking this box, you give your explicit consent under Art. 9 Abs. 2 lit. a DSGVO to these days being transferred to the calendar you have chosen. You can withdraw it at any time with effect for the future (Art. 7 Abs. 3 DSGVO) by deselecting the type again; Schichtblick then removes the already entered appointments of this type at the next sync. The lawfulness of the transfer made until the withdrawal remains unaffected, and we cannot undo what your calendar provider has stored or shared with others until then.

What Schichtblick reads. For display in the plan, Schichtblick reads only one calendar: the shared calendar you choose for this. The app does not read appointments from your other calendars, even if your operating system technically grants access to all of them. For the selection, it also reads the list of your calendars with name and account, not their content. For each appointment, the following are read: the title, start and end or the indication “all-day” (“ganztägig”), the location, the note, the reminders stored with the appointment, whether it belongs to a series, and the name and colour of the calendar it comes from. In the day sheet you see title and time; a small icon shows that a location or a note exists, and their content only appears when you open the appointment. This also includes other people's appointments, because that is what a shared calendar is. A location may contain an address, and a note may contain anything, including information about another person's health. For this very reason, Schichtblick does not store these appointments. They stay in working memory only for as long as you look at them, and they do not appear in any backup, PDF report, month image, plan for colleagues or calendar file. They never reach us. If you tap the map icon next to the location, Schichtblick passes this address to your maps app. From then on, Apple or Google is responsible itself, and their terms apply. If you set an alarm before an appointment, Schichtblick only remembers its identifier and start, not title, location or note, and excludes this information from every backup. We do not analyse their content; the only exception is the birthday detection in the next paragraph.

Birthdays. So that a cake icon can appear next to a birthday in the plan, Schichtblick compares an appointment's title with a fixed list of words (“Geburtstag”, “birthday” and a few others). On iOS, it also takes into account whether your operating system itself lists the appointment as a birthday. If the title points to a day of mourning, the icon is not shown. This check runs only on your device and only at the moment of display. The result is not stored, not analysed, not passed on and does not appear in any file; the title itself does not leave your device in the process. Schichtblick does not read your contacts or any date of birth for this. Only the wording is recognised, so a birthday without this word in the title stays without an icon.

What you enter yourself. In the family area you can create, change and delete appointments in the shared calendar. An appointment consists of a title, a time period or the indication “all-day” (“ganztägig”), a location, a note and a reminder. What you write in these fields goes unchanged into your device's calendar and thus to everyone who can see this calendar. Schichtblick does not check this content and does not keep its own copy of it. Changes and deletions take effect immediately and for everyone involved, including for appointments another person created, and we cannot undo them. A reminder is only written if you select it yourself in this appointment; otherwise, whatever is already set there stays. Appointments in a series can only be deleted here, not changed.

Recipients and transfers to third countries. Schichtblick writes only to your device's calendar storage. Nothing goes to us in the process; we do not run a server and have no access. The fact that an appointment then also sits with your calendar provider is caused by your operating system, which syncs the calendar with the account stored there. Depending on the account, Google Ireland Limited (Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland) or Apple Distribution International Ltd. (Hollyhill Industrial Estate, Hollyhill, Cork, Ireland) is itself responsible for this processing, and their privacy policies apply. Whether data is transferred to a country outside the European Union in the process, and which safeguards under Art. 44 ff. DSGVO your provider relies on, depends on that provider's terms. We have no influence on this. Further recipients are the people with whom you have shared the calendar yourself: anyone allowed to see it sees the shifts entered in it. Who you share it with is decided by you alone at your provider.

Storage period. The entered appointments remain in your calendar until you delete them there or until Schichtblick removes them at the next sync because you deleted the shift or deselected the shift type. On your device, the app permanently stores only which calendar you chose, which shift types are included, and a mapping of the appointments it created, so that no entry is created twice. It does not store other people's appointments at all. If you uninstall Schichtblick, this information disappears with the app; the appointments in your calendar remain.

How to end it again. In the family area you disconnect, after which Schichtblick no longer touches the calendar. The already entered appointments stay, because notes or invitations of their own may long since be attached to them and we do not tidy up your calendar without asking. You delete them yourself, or delete the whole calendar at your provider. You can also withdraw Schichtblick's calendar access at any time in your device's settings. Your rights under section 11 continue to apply unchanged.

Your data stays stored on your device until you delete it in the app or uninstall the app. Nothing reaches us from the app itself; it sends no data to us. We only hold the messages you write to us yourself, and the period in the previous section applies to them.

11. Your rights

Under the DSGVO, you generally have the following rights against the controller: access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and objection (Art. 21). If you have given us consent, for example by deliberately sending a backup or a screenshot (section 9) or by including sick days in the family area (section 8), you can withdraw it at any time with effect for the future (Art. 7 Abs. 3 DSGVO). This does not affect the lawfulness of the processing carried out until the withdrawal. Through the app we collect or store no personal data about you. We therefore only hold the data that comes from an email from you (section 9). Your rights relate to this data; an informal message to info@mlp3d.de is enough. For the purchase process, please contact the respective store. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Datenschutz-Aufsichtsbehörde) (Art. 77 DSGVO). The authority responsible for us is the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information Baden-Württemberg (Landesbeauftragter für den Datenschutz und die Informationsfreiheit Baden-Württemberg, www.baden-wuerttemberg.datenschutz.de). You can also contact the supervisory authority where you live.

Right to object. Insofar as we process data on the basis of Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. f DSGVO, that is, support messages outside a contract with you and the access data when you visit this website (section 13), you can object to this processing at any time on grounds relating to your particular situation (Art. 21 DSGVO). We will then no longer process your message, unless we can demonstrate compelling legitimate grounds.

12. No automated decision-making, note on the calculations

There is no automated decision-making, including profiling, within the meaning of Art. 22 DSGVO. The app's supplement and tax calculation gives a non-binding estimate (Richtwert) based on the values you enter yourself. It is not a substitute for tax advice or legal advice and makes no legal decision concerning you.
