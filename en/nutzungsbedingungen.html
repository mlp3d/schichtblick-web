2. Description of services

Schichtblick is an app for shift workers for planning shifts and for working out supplements (Zuschläge). The basic functions (creating and editing a shift calendar, shift types and shift patterns, sharing a month as an image, passing on and reading in the plan for colleagues as a file, exporting the calendar as a calendar file for other calendar apps, opening and accepting a colleague's shift swap offer, and creating and reading back a local backup) are free of charge permanently and can be used locally on your device. You need neither an account nor a purchase for this. For a one-time fee, “Schichtblick+” unlocks the extended functions, in particular the calculation of supplements and of tax-free shares (income tax only), the monthly and annual evaluation and the PDF report. The family area is also part of Schichtblick+: Schichtblick enters your shifts into a calendar that you choose yourself on your device, shows you the appointments from the shared calendar you choose in the app, and lets you create, change and delete appointments there. You create the calendar yourself at your provider, and you decide yourself who you share it with. Schichtblick does not run its own service or server for this. With Schichtblick+, a widget also shows your next shifts on the home screen, and you can pass on a single shift as a swap offer. If you accept a swap offer, Schichtblick only changes your own plan. Whether the swap is valid is something you clarify with your colleagues and your employer; the app does not report anything to anyone. The range of functions may differ depending on the platform. Before buying, you can try Schichtblick+ free of charge once for 7 days. During the trial period, the calculations, the monthly evaluation and the breakdown for the previous month and the current month are available, each relative to the current date, as well as the family area, the widget, swap offers and all Schichtblick+ input options. All other months, the PDF report and the annual evaluation are reserved for the purchase. The trial period ends by itself after 7 days and does not turn into a purchase or a subscription. After that, your entries stay saved but are locked until you make a purchase. Shifts already entered in your calendar stay there; new ones are no longer entered.

3. Estimates, not tax advice or legal advice

The calculations in Schichtblick+ provide a non-binding estimate (Richtwert) for your own orientation and for comparison with your payslip (Lohnabrechnung). They are based on the information you enter yourself (in particular base wage (Grundlohn), supplement rates and place of work). Schichtblick is not a tax adviser and not a law firm, and it provides no tax advice, no tax assistance in individual cases (steuerliche Hilfeleistung im Einzelfall) and no legal services (Rechtsdienstleistung). The app makes no statement as to whether and in what amount you are entitled to supplements in your individual case. What you are actually entitled to depends on your employment contract, your collective agreement (Tarifvertrag), any works agreement (Betriebsvereinbarung) and the statutory rules. You enter the collectively agreed supplement rates yourself; Schichtblick provides no ready-made collective agreement templates for this. The same applies to the time of payment: you set yourself when a supplement is paid out. Schichtblick makes no statement of its own about this. The app also deliberately does not reflect every company-specific special rule, and like any software, Schichtblick may contain errors. The results are therefore expressly intended as a rough comparison value and not as a reliable calculation of your pay. Only your payslip is authoritative for your pay. For tax or legal questions, please contact your payroll office, a tax adviser or another body authorised to give advice.

The same applies to your working time and your time account (Zeitkonto). If you enter a weighting of times (for example that hours from a certain time of day only count partially towards your time account), Schichtblick works solely with the rule you specify yourself. Whether this rule applies to you and how it is to be interpreted follows from your employment contract, your collective agreement and your works agreement. Only your employer's time record is authoritative for your account balance. Notices about rest periods are based solely on the shifts you have entered and on the statutory standard of 11 hours (§ 5 Abs. 1 ArbZG). The app does not know about deviations due to industry, collective agreement, works agreement or standby duty. If there is no notice, this does not mean that your rest periods have been observed.
